Step 3 of 4 — Testify
Sign Up to Testify
Committee testimony is the most powerful thing a constituent can do. Your words go on the official legislative record. Usually takes just a few minutes.
When you testify, say your name, your zip code, and why this matters to you personally. You don't need to be an expert. You just need to show up.

Step 4 of 4 — Request a Meeting
Your Representative
A formal meeting request puts you on their calendar. It's harder to ignore than a call or email. Tap to open the draft and send it.
